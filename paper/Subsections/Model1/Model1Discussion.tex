\subsection{Conclusion}
 
The dry-air balance closed in all 144 runs. \textbf{Passenger airspeed assumptions.} We assign $1.81\,\mathrm{m/s}$ at the reference passenger with the Fan and use the venue's baseline airspeed with either evaporative cooler or with cooling off. The coolers' supply airflow enters the zone mixing balance. We assign no additional passenger-level airspeed from their discharge. This assumption affects the SET and efficiency comparisons.
